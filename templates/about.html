About the platform

Landlord-Tenant Direct Connect (LT Direct)

The Landlord-Tenant Direct Connect Platform is an academic web application designed to facilitate direct contact between landlords and prospective tenants.

Key objectives

  • Allow tenants to search for and filter residential apartments.
  • Allow landlords to register and manage apartment listings.
  • Provide tenant apartment preferences and personalised recommendations using Weighted K-Nearest Neighbour (Weighted KNN).
  • Enable direct landlord–tenant messaging.
  • Support an administrative landlord-verification workflow.

How recommendations work

Your preferences form a query vector, and each apartment becomes a candidate vector. The system ranks apartments by a weighted Euclidean distance — a smaller distance means a stronger match. This is a similarity-based ranking, not a guarantee.

This is an academic prototype. It does not legally verify property ownership, process payments, or act as a legal authority.
