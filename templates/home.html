{# ── Intelligent recommendations explainer ── #}

Intelligent recommendations

The platform uses Weighted K-Nearest Neighbour (Weighted KNN) to rank apartments by how closely they match your stated preferences.

  • Your preferences form a query vector
  • Apartments are compared using a weighted distance
  • Closest matches are ranked highest for you
  • Hard constraints (e.g. maximum rent) are always respected

Results are similarity-based recommendations — not guarantees. They reflect available structured data and your stated preferences.
